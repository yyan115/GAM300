The Game

Fight with chains. Kill with precision.

Kusane is a 3D action platformer where a chain weapon and a blade carry you through waves of ground and airborne enemies. Hook a target from across the room, pull it in, and finish it on the ground, or take the fight into the air and keep it there.

Feathers from fallen enemies charge a blast that clears the field when it closes in, and a dodge through an attack slows the world long enough to answer it.

The Chain

A weapon, a way across a gap, and the only way to bring a flying enemy down to where a blade can reach it.

Aerial Combat

Launch an enemy, follow it up, and keep the combo alive between the ground and the air.

The Miniboss

Three phases that each have to be read: it telegraphs, it answers what you do, and it cannot be stunlocked.
