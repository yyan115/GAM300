The Game

A temple to walk back through, one room at a time.

You play Kusane, the last of the clan that kept a mountain temple. The Karasu took it from them in a night, and she woke in the ruins without her wings. She finds a feather chain resting on a holder and starts back through her own home: the hall, the study, the statue hall, the weapon quarters, and the room at the end, where a Karasu wearing wings made of her clan's feathers is waiting.

Five rooms and one fight at the end of them, made by a student team on an engine they wrote themselves.

  • SwordA three hit ground combo. Jump out of a swing to lift an enemy off the floor and keep hitting it in the air, ending on a slam.
  • ChainThrow it at something and it holds. A crow on the ground is dragged to you. A crow in the air is pulled down and stays on the ground for good. It does no damage of its own, and it does not aim itself.
  • FeathersEvery hit that lands knocks feathers loose. Twenty of them buys a fan of ten feathers, fired where you are aiming, each one exploding where it hits.
  • DodgeOne charge, and you are untouchable for all of it. Dodge through an attack and time drops to a sixth of normal for about half a second.
  • DoorsSome doors are chained shut. Hook one and pull until it comes off its frame.
  • HealthThirty. Two shrines to save at. Falling kills you outright.
The Chain

Crows do not come down on their own.

The Guardian Statue Hall

Six Karasu on the floor and two in the air, around the statue the room is named for. The ones in the air throw knives in threes and never land, and a sword cannot reach them.

So the chain goes out, one comes down, and it does not get back up: a flyer pulled out of the sky is a ground enemy for the rest of the fight. The feathers the fight knocks loose pay for the ten that go back out and explode.

The Boss

One fight ends the game.

The Battle Dome

Six hundred health and three phases, and it changes at two thirds and one third, so the phase you are in is a readout of how far you have got.

On the ground it claws at you, throws knives when you back off, and answers three swings, or a throw or two, by charging the length of the arena. In the air it flies a grid and fires aimed volleys, and that is the one window where the chain still works on it: hook it down and it owes you eight seconds on the floor.

Past a third it glows, which means the chain will not reach it. It drops feather bombs across the arena, marks the ground under you and dives on it, and cannot be knocked out of anything it has started.
